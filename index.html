Research focus.

My work sits at the intersection of predictive modeling, molecular dynamics, and structural biology. I build graph neural networks, ensemble models, and reproducible data pipelines to turn complex molecular data into useful, interpretable predictions.

At Procter & Gamble's UC Digital Accelerator and the University of Cincinnati, I work across chemical safety, biomolecular interactions, and drug-discovery benchmarks—using simulation when atomistic detail is needed.

Projects.

Working projects where molecular science meets machine learning.

Geometric deep learning · Featured↗

FlexENN

An interface-focused geometric graph neural network that predicts experimental binding free energies from the 3D structures of globular and intrinsically disordered protein complexes.

  • PyTorch
  • GNN
  • 3D structure
  • Binding ΔG
Molecular AI↗

OpenADMET PXR Challenge

A reproducible activity-prediction workflow combining Chemprop message-passing neural networks with classical ML, greedy ensemble selection, robust calibration, and similarity-aware correction.

  • Chemprop
  • RDKit
  • Ensemble ML
  • ADMET
Cheminformatics · Regression↗

Protein–Ligand Affinity ML

A comparative ML pipeline using Morgan fingerprints to benchmark linear, kernel, tree, and boosting models on protein–ligand binding-affinity data.

  • Scikit-learn
  • XGBoost
  • LightGBM
  • PDBbind+
Explainable AI↗

Explainable AI for Heart Disease

A machine-learning workflow that predicts heart-disease risk and uses SHAP explanations to show which clinical features drive each prediction.

  • Python
  • SHAP
